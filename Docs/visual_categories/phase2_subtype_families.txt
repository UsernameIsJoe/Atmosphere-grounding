PURPOSE
-------
Under each Phase-1 category, define SUBTYPE FAMILIES — mid-level slots that
group related visual cues. Families are NOT full Grounding DINO word lists.
Concrete phrases come later. Still NO atmosphere lexicon bindings.

A subtype family should be:
  - Visually coherent (same kind of thing)
  - Expandable into multiple detection phrases or one metric later
  - Supported by theory and/or established streetscape/public-space practice


DECISION RULES
--------------
- Prefer park/plaza relevance for this Cambridge-style open-space corpus.
- Prefer families that are photographable in still place photos.
- Soft / scene-level families allowed when theory requires them
  (tagged soft; extractors assigned in Phase 3).
- Do not invent atmosphere meanings for families.

CATEGORY → SUBTYPE FAMILIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1) objects_furnishings
   Families:
     seating              — benches, chairs, seat walls, ledges
     play                 — playground equipment, play structures
     service_amenity      — trash bins, restrooms, drinking fountains, kiosks
     bike_facilities      — bike racks, bike parking
     other_amenity        — catch-all for miscellaneous stay equipment
   Support:
     - Gehl (2010) Cities for People — Comfort: sit, stand, play/exercise.
     - Whyte (1980) Social Life of Small Urban Spaces — sittable space,
       ledges, secondary seating.
   Notes: "other_amenity" is an extensibility bucket for v1; refine later.


2) vegetation
   Families:
     tree                 — individual / countable trees
     canopy_shade_plant   — canopy/shade vegetation as cover cue
     lawn_groundcover     — grass, lawn, groundcover
     planting_bed         — flower beds, planted borders
     shrub                — shrubs / bushes
   Support:
     - Kaplan & Kaplan (1989) Experience of Nature — natural content.
     - Middel et al. (2019) Landscape & Urban Planning — trees as primary
       spherical fraction; pervious surfaces related to soft ground/plant.
     - Wang et al. (2019) arXiv:1905.04488 — streetscape vs remotely sensed
       greenery as distinct measures.
   Notes: Cover vs instance distinction reserved for Phase 3 extractors.


3) circulation_access
   Families:
     path_walkway         — paths, walkways, sidewalks
     plaza_hardscape      — open paved gathering surfaces
     stairs               — stairs, steps
     entrance_threshold   — gates as entry, entrances, thresholds
     crossing             — crosswalks / marked crossings (when present)
   Support:
     - Lynch (1960) Image of the City — paths.
     - Gehl (2010) — opportunities to walk; accessibility.
   Notes: Keep entrance_threshold here (movement) even if "gate" also appears
   under boundaries (barrier role); dual listing is OK at family level;
   phrase lists will disambiguate later.


4) boundaries_edges
   Families:
     fence
     wall
     gate
     railing
     hedge
   Support:
     - Lynch (1960) — edges.
     - Appleton (1975) — refuge / cover; spatial enclosure devices.
     - CPTED / Newman (1972) Defensible Space — territorial boundaries,
       access control (fences, gates) as environmental design elements.
   Notes: Descriptive only; no “excluding” or “safe” binding.


5) facade_built_form
   Families:
     building_mass        — building as volumetric mass in view
     facade_frontage      — facade / frontage plane
     opening              — window, door
     canopy_awning        — canopies, awnings, overhangs
   Support:
     - Gehl (1986) “Soft edges” in residential streets; Gehl et al. on
       ground-floor facades, edge effect, support effect
       (Close encounters with buildings).
     - Gehl (2010) — human scale; building edges of public space.
     - Middel et al. (2019) — buildings as composition class.
   Notes: Soft-edge theory motivates openings/canopies as frontage detail,
   not atmosphere labels.


6) light
   Families:
     light_fixture        — lamps, street lights, park lights, bollard lights
     lit_area_soft        — soft cue: lit path / illuminated area (phrase-level)
     brightness_level     — global brightness / luminance statistic
   Support:
     - Böhme (1993; 2017) — light as atmosphere generator.
     - Gehl public-life quality practice — lighting under protection/comfort.
   Notes: Separate fixtures (objects) from illumination quality (properties).


7) materiality_surface
   Families:
     paving_type          — asphalt, concrete, brick paving, stone paving, gravel
     soft_ground          — dirt, mulch, sand (non-lawn soft surfaces)
     hard_vertical        — brick / stone / glass / metal / wood as wall-like
                            surface materials
     water_surface        — water as surface material (when not a “feature object”)
   Support:
     - Böhme — material ecstasies / generators.
     - Zumthor (2006) Atmospheres — materials and surfaces.
     - Middel et al. (2019) — impervious vs pervious surface fractions.
   Notes: water_surface overlaps water_terrain; keep both: materiality =
   surface property; water_terrain = landscape feature identity.


8) water_terrain
   Families:
     fountain
     water_body           — pond, lake, river, pool (natural/constructed body)
     slope_grade          — visible slope / grade change
     rock_landform        — rocks, ledges as landform (not seating ledge)
   Support:
     - Preference / restorative literature: water content (Kaplan & Kaplan 1989
       and related preference studies).
     - Whyte (1980) — fountains as plaza attractors / sittable edges around
       water features.
   Notes: slope_grade is harder photographically; keep as optional family.


9) social_presence
   Families:
     people
     bicycle
     gathering_cluster_soft — soft cue for groups / gathering (phrase/count)
   Support:
     - Jacobs (1961) eyes on the street.
     - Whyte (1980); Gehl (2010) public life.
     - Middel et al. (2019) non-permanent objects (people/vehicles).
   Notes: No demographic recognition goals; presence/activity traces only.


10) spatial_structure
    Families:
      sky_openness         — sky fraction / view factor
      building_enclosure   — building fraction / view factor
      vegetation_enclosure — tree/vegetation fraction as spatial cover
      ground_openness      — visible ground / open floor fraction
    Support:
      - Appleton (1975); Dosen & Ostwald (2016) prospect–refuge meta-analysis.
      - Middel et al. (2019) spherical fractions: sky, trees, buildings,
        impervious, pervious.
      - GSV SVF/TVF/BVF literature (e.g. sky/tree/building view factors).
    Notes: These are AMOUNT dimensions, not object inventories.


11) composition_contrast
    Families:
      luminance_contrast   — contrast / dynamic range proxies
      color_variety        — color entropy / palette diversity
      edge_complexity      — edge density / structural complexity
      focal_dominance_soft — soft: whether a strong focal object dominates
    Support:
      - Kaplan (1979); Kaplan & Kaplan (1989) — coherence & complexity.
      - Machado et al. / computerized visual complexity — edge density as
        predictor of perceived complexity
        (https://cdv.dei.uc.pt/wp-content/uploads/publications-cdv/mrnscc2015.pdf).
      - Color information entropy linked to landscape preference ratings
        (HortTechnology 2015, 25(5):625).
      - Aesthetics Toolbox (Behavior Research Methods, 2025) — standardized
        lightness/color/complexity/entropy image properties.
        https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-025-02632-3
    Notes: Families are metric-oriented; phrase lists optional via SigLIP.


12) wayfinding_information
    Families:
      sign
      landmark_object      — distinctive landmark-like object in scene
      map_board            — map boards / information boards
    Support:
      - Lynch (1960) landmarks.
      - Kaplan legibility (1979; 1989).
    Notes: Small family set by design; expand phrases later if needed.
